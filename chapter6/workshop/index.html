    Think 2026 Activation Agent

    Overview

    The Think 2026 Activation Agent helps you turn Think 2026 themes into client‑ready assets. Continue the Think 2026 momentum by tailoring the core themes to the business initiatives of IBM’s clients—all in one place. IBM sellers can share and reuse these assets with their clients, aligning opportunities to key innovations emerging from Think 2026’s five core themes:

    • AI Native Developer
    • Identity in the Age of Agentic
    • Hybrid Operations in the AI Era
    • Sovereign AI by Design
    • Activating Enterprise Data for AI

    Call to action

    Keep the momentum going. Lead the conversation.

    Following the wrap-up of Think 2026, we encourage every IBM seller to share both a tailored Presentation and an Interactive Podcast with their clients. Repeat this across multiple ISC opportunities to maintain momentum throughout 2026.

    1. Within the Agent, input your client ISC opportunity or account number
    2. Download and personalize a Think 2026 deck aligned to the client’s goals
    3. Ask questions in the agent’s chat for suggestions on how to align a Think 2026 theme with the ISC opportunity
    4. Generate and tailor an Interactive Podcast that captures the theme’s core values in 5 minutes or less
    5. Share with your clients

    The steps are simple — and detailed instructions for how to do so are detailed further below.

    Step 1: Add an ISC opportunity or account number

    Under Client Data, users can directly integrate IBM Sales Cloud (ISC) data into their Workshop session context. This will pull all relevant account or opportunity details into the interactions users have with the agent's chat and tooling, tightly aligning the responses Workshop returns with the full history of the client opportunity and relationship.

    Included with the ISC opportunity context are key client data points, product and solution context, sales enablement assets, and integrated insights. ISC integration allows each watsonx Workshop session to be guided by the most relevant, accurate, and up-to-date— literally, up to the day —data available.

    1. Within the Think 2026 Activation Agent, locate the Context panel on the left-hand side of the page. Click the Client Data[A] button as indicated.


    2. A Configure client data window will appear. From here, you can use the ISC ID Type[B] drop-down menu to select between either an ISC Opportunity or Account number.

    3. After making a selection, input your ISC number and click Search[C] to pull up the linked ISC client records.

    4. When ready, click Configure Session[D] to automatically add the full ISC record to the agent's context window.


    These actions are simple to perform, but tremendously powerful. With your opportunity's ISC records added to Workshop's context, all interactions with the Think 2026 Activation Agent from this point onward are directly informed by IBM's full history and account details related to that particular client. This ensures engagements you make with the Chat are contextually relevant— with respect to the client's business objectives —and aligned with IBM's Think 2026 themes.

    Step 2: Download and personalize a Think 2026 deck aligned to the client’s goals

    1. Use the Change Theme[A] context selector to instantly shift the agent's expertise between the five core themes[B]. The theme can be changed at any point during your session and the relevant context will adjust automatically.


    2. Just below (in the left-hand Context panel) is a list of client-ready PPT assets aligned to the specific Think 2026 chosen. Changing the selected theme will dynamically update the list of pre-approved assets.


    3. Click any of the links to navigate to the Seismic source page.

    4. Download the asset so that you can further tailor the messaging to your ISC opportunity.

    Step 3: Customize the asset for your ISC opportunity

    Within the central part of your interface is the Chat panel, which is now fully informed about the context of your ISC opportunity.

    Toward the bottom of the panel are the interactive Skills, which have been handcrafted ahead of time with best-practice prompts designed for rapidly garnering insightful, targeted responses from the LLM. The agent for Think 2026 Activation has been prepared with Skills around the following topics:

    • Practice an executive pitch
    • Roleplay a client conversation
    • Summarize key technical updates
    • Practice handling objections

    Use these "quick-start" prompts to bypass complex prompting and jump into high-impact tasks.

    1. Within the Chat, using either the prepared Skills[A] or entirely novel prompts of your own choosing, start asking questions about your selected Think 2026 theme and how to relate it to the needs and business goals of your ISC opportunity.


    2. As you leverage the Agent's contextualized view of both the Think 2026 theme and the ISC opportunity data to tailor a bespoke message for your client, be sure to customize one of the client-ready PPT assets (if you so choose) with that messaging.

    Step 4: Generate and tailor an Interactive Podcast

    watsonx Workshop’s Interactive Podcast transforms Think 2026 core themes into a multi-person audio discussion that your can clients to engage with on the go.

    Interactive Podcasts are customizable in length and focus. For your purposes, keep the duration to 5 minutes or less. In terms of focus and content, you can opt to either generate the Podcast with the default prompt (which has been tuned specifically for Think 2026 messaging) or further customize it with your own client's details.

    You will need to download the Interactive Podcast and send it directly to the client, packaged alongside the tailored Think 2026 theme PPT deck you prepared in Step 3.

    1. To get started, click Interactive Podcast[A] within the Studio panel (on the right-hand side of the interface) to open the Generate Podcast menu.


    2. Keep the Podcast Length[B] to a Default duration of approximately 5 minutes.

    3. Customize your Podcast Title[C] to the ISC opportunity.

    4. Consider further customizing the What should the AI host focus on?[D] prompt with details specific to your ISC opportunity: the client's business or executive's name, their industry, and any other salient points you'd like to structure the Podcast's talking points around.

    5. When satisfied, click Generate Podcast[E] to kick off the workstream. On average, these podcasts will need a few minutes or longer to generate. You can track the progression and completition status from the Studio panel.


    Step 5: Share with your clients

    You're ready to go — now keep the Think 2026 momentum rolling by sharing both the tailored Presentation and the Interactive Podcast with your client. Repeat this across multiple ISC opportunities. Create bespoke messaging for every client's business with Workshop and accelerate your opportunities.
